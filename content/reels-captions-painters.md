## Production notes

- **Placement:** upper-middle third of the frame, in a large bold sans font with an outline or shadow.
- **Timing:** change beats on a visual action (a brush stroke, a tape pull) whenever you can.
- **Payoff:** hold the final beat about 1 second longer than the rest.
- **Length:** keep it to 7–15 seconds total so the loop back to the start feels natural and people rewatch.
