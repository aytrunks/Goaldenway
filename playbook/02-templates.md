# Step 2: Create 5 templates

> Simple, repeatable systems that do 80% of what the customer wants. Then you customize the remaining 20% per client.

A template is a **product**, not a project. It has a name, a fixed scope, a price, and a delivery
timeline, and it gets built from the same blueprint every time.

## The 5 templates

| # | Template | Buyer | Pain it removes | Starter price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | [AI Inbox & Support Triage](../templates/02-inbox-triage.md) | E-com, SaaS, agencies | Hours a day spent sorting and answering the same emails | $2,000 |

Why these five:

- Each one ties to **revenue or hours saved**, so it's easy to justify the price.
- They share the same core: form or webhook in, then AI, then CRM, then notification. What you learn building one carries over to the rest.
- Each one naturally leads to a retainer (monitoring, tuning, new use cases). See [Step 4](04-retain-and-ascend.md).

## The 80/20 split

For every template, write down which parts are fixed and which are customized:

| Fixed 80% (the blueprint) | Custom 20% (per client) |
| --- | --- |
| Scenario structure and module order | Their CRM/tool connections |
| Prompt skeleton and output schema | Their tone of voice, FAQs, offers |
| Airtable base schema | Field names that match their language |
| Error handling and alerting | Business rules (routing, hours, territories) |
| Loom walkthrough and SOP | Brand assets and email copy |

If a client asks for something that isn't in the blueprint, quote it as an add-on. Don't absorb it.

## Build each template once, properly

For each template in `templates/`:

1. Build it for a **fictional client** in your own accounts.
2. Export the blueprint (Make → *Export Blueprint*, or n8n → *Download*) into `templates/blueprints/`.
3. Record a 3-minute demo Loom. This is your #1 sales asset.
4. Write the client intake questions (they're already in each spec).
5. Time yourself on the second build. Aim for **under 6 hours** of delivery per client.

## Delivery timeline (every template)
