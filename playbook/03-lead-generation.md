# Step 3: Generate leads

> Cold email infrastructure, communities, and Upwork/Fiverr profiles, all running at the same time.

Run all three channels in parallel from week one. Each one fills the gaps the others leave:

| Channel | Speed to first $ | Cost | Ceiling | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Upwork / Fiverr | Fast (days to weeks) | Connects and fees | Medium | First cash and testimonials |
| Communities | Medium | Time | Medium | Trust and referrals |
| Cold email | Slow start (2–3 weeks of warmup) | $150–300/mo | High | Scale and predictable volume |

## Pick a niche for outbound

Keep Upwork broad, but make cold email **niche-specific**. Pick one vertical and one template:

> "We set up an AI system that texts every new {{roofing}} lead within 60 seconds and books the hot ones on your calendar."

A good niche has clear revenue per job (more than $1K), runs paid ads or gets inbound calls, and has owners who are reachable by email.

## A. Cold email infrastructure

### Setup (one time, about $150–300/month)

1. **Domains:** Buy 3–5 lookalike domains (for example `getgoaldenway.com` and `trygoaldenway.com`). Never send cold email from your main domain.
2. **Inboxes:** Create 2–3 per domain on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, for 6–15 inboxes in total.
3. **DNS:** Set up SPF, DKIM, and DMARC (`p=none` to start). Forward each domain to your main site.
4. **Warmup:** Warm up for 14–21 days in Instantly or Smartlead before any cold sending.
5. **Volume:** Send at most 30–40 cold emails per inbox per day. 10 inboxes × 30 = 300/day, about 6,000/month.
6. **Lists:** Build them with [Template 4](../templates/04-lead-gen-engine.md). You're selling what you use.

### Sequence (3 steps, plain text, no links in email 1)

**Email 1 (day 0)**
```
Subject: {{first_name}}, quick question

{{personalized_opener}}

Most {{niche}} owners I talk to lose leads because nobody calls back within the first
few minutes. We build a system that texts and emails every new lead in under 60 seconds
and books the hot ones straight onto your calendar.

Worth a 2-min video showing how it'd work for {{company}}?

{{sender_first_name}}
```

**Email 2 (day 3)**
```
{{first_name}}, one number: we set this up for a {{similar_business}} and they booked
{{result}} extra appointments in the first month, with no new ad spend.

Want me to send the walkthrough?
```

**Email 3 (day 7)**
```
Last note. If lead follow-up isn't a priority right now, no worries.
If it is, reply "video" and I'll record a quick one for {{company}}.
```

### Reply handling

- Positive reply: send a **personalized Loom** (a 2-minute demo of the template with their logo or site), then a booking link.
- Track results in the dashboard: sends, positive replies, calls booked.
- Benchmarks to beat: 40%+ open rate if you're tracking it (turn tracking off when you can), 1–3% positive replies.

## B. Upwork / Fiverr

### Profile

**Title:** `AI Automation Expert | Make.com, n8n, Zapier | CRM & Lead Systems`

**Overview (first 2 lines are all that show, so make them count):**
```
I build AI automations that save businesses 10–40 hours a week, from instant lead
follow-up to AI inbox triage, client onboarding, and voice receptionists.

What I can set up for you in about 7 days:
• Speed-to-lead: text + email every new lead in 60 seconds, auto-book calls
• AI email triage: classify, draft, and auto-reply to routine support email
• Client onboarding: contract → invoice → folders → project → welcome, hands-free
• Lead gen engine: scraped, enriched, personalized prospects straight into your CRM
• AI receptionist: answers calls 24/7 and books appointments

Every build includes a Loom walkthrough, documentation, and 30 days of support.
```

**Portfolio:** Add the 5 template demo Looms, each with a before/after in one sentence.

**Fiverr gigs:** Make one gig per template, each with three tiers (Basic is the template as-is, Standard adds 1 integration, Premium adds everything plus a 30-day optimization period).

### Proposal formula (under 120 words)

```
Hi {{name}}, I've built this exact workflow before: {{one-line restatement of their
problem in their words}}.

Here's how I'd approach it:
1. {{step}}
2. {{step}}
3. {{step}}

Here's a 2-min video of a similar system I built: {{loom}}

Quick question so I can scope it: {{one smart question about their setup}}?

{{name}}
```

### Rules

- Apply within **1 hour** of a job posting. Speed wins on Upwork. A saved search with an alert (or a VA) handles this.
- Apply to 5–10 relevant jobs a day in the first month.
- Price your first 3 jobs lower to earn reviews, then raise to template pricing.
- Move repeat clients onto a retainer contract. On Upwork, weekly hourly or monthly fixed contracts both work.

## C. Communities

Choose **2** communities and go deep, rather than trying 10:

- Skool, Discord, or Slack groups for your niche's **business owners**, not other automation builders.
- Reddit: r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, and niche subreddits. Answer questions, don't pitch.
- LinkedIn: post 3 times a week. Show a build (screenshot plus the result) and tell a client story.
- Local business groups, the chamber of commerce, BNI.

**Daily 30-minute routine:** Answer 3 questions with real value, share 1 build, and DM anyone who asked about automation.
