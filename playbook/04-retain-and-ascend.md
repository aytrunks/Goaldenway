# Step 4: Retain and ascend

> This is where the real money lives. Many of the most lucrative clients start with a $1K one-and-done project
> and end up paying $50K+ over the course of a year.

Getting a new client is expensive, and expanding an existing one is almost free. **Every project is a first step toward a retainer.**

## The package ladder

| Rung | Offer | Price | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Free audit: a 30-min call plus a Loom of 3 automation opportunities | $0 | Build trust and map the account |
| 1 | **Starter**: one template, installed as-is | $1,000–1,500 | Quick win and proof |
| 2 | **System**: template plus customization, integrations, and 1–2 add-ons | $3,000–7,500 | Solve a whole workflow |
| 3 | **Growth retainer**: monitoring, tuning, 1 new automation a month, monthly report | $1,500–2,500/mo | Recurring revenue |
| 4 | **Scale retainer**: fractional "Head of Automation", unlimited small requests, quarterly roadmap | $3,500–6,000/mo | Become part of the business |

Path to $50K/year from one client: $1.5K Starter → $5K System (month 2) → $2.5K/mo Growth × 3 → $6K/mo Scale × 6 = **$50K**.

## When to ascend

| Moment | Move |
| --- | --- |
| Day 6: Handoff call | Show the results so far. Ask: "What's the next most annoying manual process in your business?" Write down the answer. |
| Day 21: Hypercare check-in | Show the numbers (hours saved, leads responded to). Pitch the Growth retainer using the list from day 6. |
| Monthly report | Every report ends with "Next month I recommend building ___." |
| Quarterly review | Present a 90-day automation roadmap. If they have 3 or more items, pitch the Scale tier. |
| Any new request | Never quote it as a one-off if they're on a retainer. Fold it in, which shows the retainer's value. |

## Retainer pitch script (day 21)

```
"Quick recap: since we launched, the system has handled {{X}} leads/emails/calls and saved
roughly {{Y}} hours. That's about ${{Z}} a month in time.

Two things usually happen next: things change (new lead sources, new offers, API changes)
and you'll think of more to automate. You mentioned {{their day-6 answer}}.

Most clients move onto a monthly plan: I monitor and tune everything, fix anything that
breaks within 24h, and build one new automation every month. It's ${{price}}/month,
month-to-month. Want me to start with {{their day-6 answer}}?"
```

## The monthly report (automate it)

The retainer is only renewed if the client *sees* the value. Send it on the 1st of every month:

1. **Headline number**: hours saved or revenue influenced this month.
2. **What ran**: executions, success rate, errors caught and fixed.
3. **What we shipped**: new automations and improvements.
4. **Next month**: the recommended build. This line is the ascension ask.

Generate it with a scheduled scenario that pulls execution counts and Airtable metrics, has Claude write the summary, and emails a PDF.

## Retention rules

- Respond within **4 business hours**. Fix breakages within **24 hours**.
- Use proactive error alerts: you should know something broke before the client does.
- Never let a retainer month go by without shipping something visible.
- Ask for a referral right after a win: "Who else do you know who deals with ___?"
- Track the **net revenue retention** metric in the dashboard. The target is over 120%.
