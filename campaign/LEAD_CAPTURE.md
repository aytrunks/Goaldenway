# Lead Capture & Handoff Workflow

Your job: collect leads and hand them to sales. Not sell. Not give estimates. Collect and hand off.

## The setup

**One unified text inbox.** Every DM that comes in through Instagram/Facebook? Text them back at a single Google Voice number (or Twilio, or similar). One number, one inbox, one person monitoring it — you.

1. **Get a Google Voice number** (free, takes 5 min) — this is your dedicated leads line
2. **Add it to your Instagram and Facebook bios** — "Text us for info" with the number
3. **In the campaign hub:** open "Lead capture & handoff" and fill in the actual number (replaces the placeholder)
4. When DMs come in: tap "+1 DM" in the hub, text them back immediately with a handoff script

## The scripts (copy these into the hub)

All four are in the campaign hub as collapsible quick-reference. Use whichever applies:

**First reply when they DM:**
> "Hey! Glad you got in touch. We'd love to chat. Stop by the office and I'll connect you with our sales team — they'll get you set up."

**If they ask about pricing:**
> "Great question. Our sales team handles all pricing based on your specific boat and needs. Come by or book a time, and they'll give you the full breakdown."

**If they ask about turnaround time:**
> "Depends on the size and current condition. Come by and the team can give you an exact timeline. Usually 1-3 days for a full detail."

**If they want to book now:**
> "Perfect! Come by the office at [address] and we'll get you on the schedule. Or I can connect you with the team to line up a time. See you soon!"

## Why this works

- **You stay fast** — text replies come in faster than Instagram comment chains
- **Sales owns the conversion** — you triage, they close. Not your job to negotiate price or timeline
- **No back-and-forth** — "come by the office" is the default answer to everything. If they can't, sales team books them
- **Unified record** — every lead lives in one text inbox, not scattered across three apps

## The flow

```
DM comes in on Instagram
    ↓
You see it, tap "+1 DM" in hub
    ↓
Text them back with appropriate script
    ↓
They either: come by office, or ask for a call
    ↓
Sales team takes over
```

That's it. Two taps, one text, move on to the next boat.
