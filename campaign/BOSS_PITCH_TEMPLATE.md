# Endgame Presentation — For Boss

Strategy: show proof first (metrics + real client leads), then ask for the marketing role.
Everything below should be pulled straight from the **Boss Presentation Summary** tab in
`Goaldenway_Boathouse_Tracker.xlsx` after Day 7 — don't estimate, screenshot the real numbers.

## What to show

- Total views, engagement rate, DMs received (7-day test)
- "X people asked to book after seeing videos" — this is the **Booking-intent DMs** cell in the
  tracker, which is a manual tag: read through the DM log and count only the ones that were
  actual booking/quote asks, not just "nice boat" comments
- Hook performance chart — which type won, Value vs. Reynolds-style (Hook Bank tab)
- Format performance chart — which format converted best (Format Comparison tab)
- Watch time data — proof people aren't just scrolling past
- Growth trajectory — views per post, Day 1 vs. Day 7

## The pitch

The tracker auto-writes this line from your real Day 7 numbers (Boss Presentation Summary tab,
bottom cell):

> "Here's what I built in 7 days solo. Hooks are performing at **[X]%** watch time. Format
> **[Y]** is our DM driver. We're getting inbound — **[Z]** DMs so far. Let's make this official
> and scale it."

Bring:
1. Phone with the actual Instagram posts pulled up
2. Printed or screenshotted **Boss Presentation Summary** tab
3. Printed or screenshotted **Format Comparison** and **Hook Bank** tabs as backup detail if asked

## Reminder

This is a proof-first pitch — lead with the numbers, not the ask. Only bring up the Marketing
Manager role after the data has landed.
