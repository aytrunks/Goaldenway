# Endgame Presentation — For Boss

Strategy: show proof first (real client leads), then ask for the marketing role.

This got a lot simpler than the original plan. You don't need charts or a spreadsheet review —
open the **campaign hub**, tap "Copy a quick update for your boss," and you've got the pitch
line: *"Posted content from N boats on Instagram so far, M DMs in."* That's the whole proof.

## What to show

- Your phone, with the actual Instagram posts pulled up — the boats, the before/afters
- That one line: how many boats got content, how many DMs came in
- A couple of the DMs themselves if any were real booking/quote asks — that's the strongest
  proof there is, stronger than any metric

## The pitch

> "Here's what I built solo, no budget, just my phone. Posted content from **[N]** boats.
> **[M]** DMs in. Let's make this official and scale it."

## Reminder

Lead with the numbers, not the ask. Only bring up the Marketing Manager role after the boats-and-DMs
count has landed. If you want deeper analysis later (which hook, which format actually converts),
that's what `Goaldenway_Boathouse_Tracker.xlsx` is for — it's still in the repo, untouched, for
whenever there's enough volume to make that breakdown worth building.
